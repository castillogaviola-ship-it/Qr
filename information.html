🥞 ABOUT BYTECAKE

ByteCake offers soft and fluffy mini pancakes with delicious fillings, made especially for GIST students, teachers, and staff.

📍 LOCATION

Gateways Institute of Science and Technology (GIST)

Available inside the school.

🕐 OPERATING TIME

Monday–Friday

During break time, lunch time, and other permitted operating hours.

🛒 HOW TO ORDER

Choose your favorite pancake flavor, send us your order through our page, and wait for our confirmation.

Pick up your order inside GIST during the agreed time.
